## Why this repository?

This repo is focused on the mindset behind good architecture, not memorizing buzzwords or drawing random diagrams.

The core idea is simple:

> Requirements → Constraints → Trade-offs → Architecture → Failure Handling → Scalability

By working through this material, you will learn how to:

- reason about user and business requirements
- identify bottlenecks and system constraints
- compare design trade-offs clearly
- design for scale, reliability, and resilience
- approach interviews and architecture discussions with structure

## Suggested learning approach

### Foundations first

Build a strong understanding of:

- the actual problem being solved
- the constraints that matter most
- the trade-offs that are acceptable
- what happens when the system fails

### Then move to complexity

Once the basics are clear, move on to deeper topics such as:

- caching and read/write optimization
- load balancing and traffic distribution
- databases and partitioning
- message queues and asynchronous processing
- high availability and fault tolerance
- distributed systems and observability

---

## Learning mindset

A strong system designer asks:

- What is the real problem?
- Who are the users?
- What are the critical requirements?
- What are the bottlenecks?
- What can fail?
- Why is this design better than another option?

This mindset matters more than memorizing a single answer.
