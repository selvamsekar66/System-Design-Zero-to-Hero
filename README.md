# System Design Zero to Hero 

A practical, structured journey to learn **System Design** and **Software Architecture** from fundamentals to real-world systems.

This repository is designed for engineers who want to build strong intuition for how large-scale systems work, how architects reason, and how to answer real system design questions with confidence.

---

## Why this repository?

This repo focuses on the mindset behind good architecture rather than memorizing buzzwords or drawing random diagrams.

The key idea is simple:

> Requirements → Constraints → Trade-offs → Architecture → Failure Handling → Scalability

You will learn how to:

- reason about user and business requirements
- identify system constraints and bottlenecks
- compare trade-offs between design choices
- design for scale, reliability, and resilience
- approach real-world interviews and architecture discussions with structure

1. Start with [Day 1 — System Design Fundamentals.md](Day%201%20%E2%80%94%20System%20Design%20Fundamentals.md).

## Suggested learning approach

### Foundations first

Build a strong understanding of:

- what problem you are solving
- what constraints matter most
- which trade-offs are acceptable
- what happens when the system fails

### Then move to complexity

Once the basics are clear, move to deeper topics such as:

- caching and read/write optimization
- load balancing and traffic distribution
- databases and partitioning
- message queues and async processing
- high availability and fault tolerance
- distributed systems and observability

---

## Learning mindset

A good system designer asks:

- What is the real problem?
- Who are the users?
- What are the critical requirements?
- What are the bottlenecks?
- What can fail?
- Why is this design better than another option?

That mindset matters more than memorizing a single answer.
